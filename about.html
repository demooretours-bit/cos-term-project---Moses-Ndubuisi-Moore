Career Aspirations: Cybersecurity & DevSecOps Engineering

My immediate focus as a Computer Science student at MIVA Open University is mastering the foundational intersection of software engineering and defensive cybersecurity. Rather than viewing security as an external checklist applied at the end of development, I am building my career around DevSecOps and Application Security (AppSec)—ensuring that web applications, cloud infrastructure, and digital platforms are architected to be resilient against threats from the very first line of code.

Short-Term Academic & Technical Milestones

  • Secure Full-Stack Development: Mastering modern web technologies while mitigating OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities such as XSS, CSRF, and injection flaws.
  • Network & System Hardening: Building hands-on fluency in Linux administration, network protocol analysis, and defensive architecture.
  • Industry Certifications: Working toward CompTIA Security+ and practical lab benchmarks on TryHackMe and Hack The Box.

Mid-Term Career Trajectory

Following graduation, I aim to step into an Application Security Engineer or Security Operations Analyst role within a technology-driven enterprise or fintech organization. My focus will be conducting vulnerability assessments, integrating automated security testing into CI/CD pipelines, and partnering with engineering teams to remediate risks before production deployment.

Long-Term Vision

Over the long term, I aspire to become a Principal Cloud Security Architect leading enterprise zero-trust strategy and resilient digital infrastructure. As digital adoption expands across Nigeria and West Africa, my goal is to safeguard critical systems and user data at scale while mentoring emerging cybersecurity professionals.
